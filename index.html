About Us

We Are Ceazers 3D

Ceazers 3D is an engineering-focused design and prototyping studio. We exist to close the gap between ideas and reality, giving individuals, students, startups, and businesses access to professional-grade design and manufacturing capability.

We are not a generic print shop. We are technically capable, detail-driven engineers who understand that every project has real-world requirements, tolerances, materials, function, and fit. That understanding shapes everything we produce.

Whether you have a polished brief or just a rough idea drawn on paper, we have the tools and the expertise to take it forward. Our goal is simple: deliver work that functions, looks professional, and makes your project succeed.

What We Print With

FDM Materials

We work with a range of engineering-grade thermoplastics. Each material has unique properties suited to different applications, environments, and performance requirements. Need finer detail than FDM allows? We also offer resin printing for small, high-detail parts with a smooth finish.

PLA
PLA
Polylactic Acid

The most popular FDM material. Easy to print, great surface finish, biodegradable. Ideal for prototypes, models, and decorative parts.

Easy to Print Eco-Friendly Fine Detail

Polyethylene Terephthalate Glycol

Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene

We accept STL, OBJ, STEP, and IGES files - STL is the most common and recommended format. If you only have a sketch or concept, our CAD team can model the part from scratch. Submit files via our contact form or share a link to Google Drive, WeTransfer, or OneDrive.

Most standard parts are completed within 2–5 business days. Turnaround depends on size, complexity, and queue. Small parts under 100 mm often print overnight. Rush orders (1–2 day turnaround) are available - contact us to confirm availability. Nationwide courier delivery adds 1–3 days.

We print in PLA, PLA+, PETG, ABS, ASA, TPU (flexible), and Nylon PA12. PLA suits prototypes and display models; PETG handles chemical exposure; ABS/ASA offer UV and heat resistance; TPU produces rubber-like flexible parts; Nylon delivers top-tier strength for load-bearing components. We also offer resin printing for small, high-detail parts with a smooth finish. See our Materials section for full specs.

Prices start from around R150–R400 for small parts and scale with volume, material, and infill. Use our instant cost estimator for a ballpark - it takes under a minute. Bulk orders of 5+ units get 10% off and 10+ units get 20% off. Final quotes are confirmed after reviewing your file.

Absolutely. Our CAD design service works from sketches, photos, or verbal descriptions. We use Fusion 360, SOLIDWORKS, and Onshape to create precise, print-ready 3D models. You receive the final STL and STEP files - you own the design outright. This is popular with entrepreneurs, students, and businesses launching new products.

No minimum - we print single units. Whether you need one prototype or a batch of 50 identical parts, we accommodate both. Larger batches naturally benefit from our bulk pricing starting at 5 units. We're equally happy helping a student with one part as a business ordering in volume.
